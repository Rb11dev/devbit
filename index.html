Process

A simple five-step process.

Clear steps from the first conversation to going live, so you always know what happens next.

  1. 01

    Discuss

    We talk through your goals, your audience and what the website needs to do.

  2. 02

    Plan

    We agree the structure, features, design direction and scope before building starts.

  3. 03

    Build

    The website and its functionality are developed, with progress shared along the way.

  4. 04

    Review

    You review the work and give feedback; revisions are made within the agreed scope.

  5. 05

    Launch

    Domain and hosting are connected, everything is tested and the site goes live.
